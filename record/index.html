NetComposer / the record

The Composition is the score of the piece; the record is the score of the performance.

Why it exists

Each performance carries an exact account of itself: every sound, its contributor, license, source URL and checksum, where it was placed in the piece, every Performer setting and the seed. CC BY requires credit anyway. The record makes credit exact rather than a footnote.

Recorded music has mostly been a sealed artifact. Sample-level credit is rare and usually private. Here credit, provenance and method travel with the music, in public, readable by people and agents.

Peter’s Notarism is about witnessing and recording existence. The record is NetComposer’s act of witness.

What it makes possible

  • Reproduction: the inputs (with checksums), every setting and the seed are enough to rebuild a performance exactly.
  • Forks: change one setting or swap one Supplier, and the new record shows exactly what changed.
  • Supplier view: Suppliers can see where their sounds were used. A usage view built from records is in draft for musicmatter.art.
  • Comparison: performances of one Composition can be compared sound by sound and setting by setting.
  • The crawl path as artwork: the walk record traces every hop from the seed sound, drawn as a path chart.
  • Agents: agents can read, audit and build on any performance from the raw JSON.
  • Signed records, later: records are unsigned for now. Signing is open decision #33.

Anatomy, from a real record

Every excerpt below is copied from records/bwv846-walk.json, performance 4 (the crawl walk: 290 sounds from 234 contributors were used, 265 sounds from 209 contributors are heard; the rest lent rhythm only). Long lists are cut where marked …. The format is a draft: record_type is netcomposer.performance_record, and fields may change.

1. Top level

record_type, record_format_version, record_format_note, performance, hacks_chain, sounds_used, summary, derived_inputs, attribution, provenance_files, created_at, signature, record_hash, amendments.

performance holds what was played and how; sounds_used holds every input; summary holds the counts; record_hash, signature and amendments hold integrity.

2. Which Composition

The record names the Composition file the Performer read and pins it by sha256, so a changed text cannot pass as the same input. Later copies of that file have a different checksum. The pinned text is kept, and netcomposer.json lists it under prior next to the current sha256.

8. Integrity

record_hash is the sha256 of the record in canonical JSON (sorted keys, no whitespace) without record_hash and signature. Anyone can recompute it. Records are unsigned for now (open decision #33). When a record changes after the fact, an amendment says what changed and keeps the previous hash.
