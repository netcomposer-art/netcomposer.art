# The A♭ major Prelude, harmony only (after Bach, BWV 862)

**Composer of this Composition:** Peter · **License out:** CC BY-SA 4.0
**Meter:** 3/4 · **Tempo:** quarter note ≈ 80 BPM · **Length:** 44 bars, 40 chords
**Directionality:** 0.3 (stay close to the chords; textures are welcome if the harmony is right)
**Allowed transforms:** trim, gain, fade (no pitch-shift, no time-stretch)

## Prose

A sunny dance in three. Open, warm and lightly sprung, walking the circle of fifths. It goes out to
E-flat (bars 9–21), turns home (bars 22–25), and leans to D-flat (bars 26–32), the warmest stretch.
The home cadence comes back more and more certain, and the final A-flat is held. Daylight, not a cathedral.

Two bars on one chord: Bb7 (bars 18–19), Eb7/Bb (bars 22–23), Ab7 (bars 24–25) and Eb7 (bars 33–34).
