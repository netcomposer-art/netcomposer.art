# The E♭ minor Prelude, harmony only (after Bach, BWV 853)

**Composer of this Composition:** Peter · **License out:** CC BY-SA 4.0
**Meter:** 3/2 (six quarter beats a bar) · **Tempo:** quarter note ≈ 84 BPM · **Length:** 40 bars
**Directionality:** 0.3 (stay close to the chords; textures are welcome if the harmony is right)
**Allowed transforms:** trim, gain, fade (no pitch-shift, no time-stretch)

## Prose

Night music. A slow sarabande step, grave and close. Soft attacks, and let each chord ring into the next.
One chord per bar. The Neapolitan at bar 26, a deceptive cadence at bar 29, and four bars on one
diminished seventh while its bass moves (bars 32–35) are the peak, so let it hang. An E-flat pedal from
bar 36. It ends on E-flat major, held. A slight broadening over the last three bars. A stone church at
night, never washed out.

C-flat, F-flat and C-flat major seven are Bach's spelling. The covered chords are B, E and Bmaj7.
