How NetComposer works

Music performed by the open internet.

The idea

A Composition is plain words: the chords, the length, the mood. A Performer turns it into music using only openly licensed sounds. Every Performance comes with a public record of every sound used, where it came from and who made it.

Four roles

Anyone can take any of them.

  • Suppliers share sounds under an open license.
  • Composers write Compositions in plain words.
  • Performers (people, or the agents they send) turn a Composition into a Performance.
  • Audience listens, shares and remixes.

How it goes

  1. Write a Composition on Create.
  2. Hear it: Play sketches the chords on the same page.
  3. Perform it with the open-source Performer kit, or hand it to an agent with the Performer skill. Performing right on Create is coming soon.
  4. Share the Performance with its record.

The rules

  • Sounds in: CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or public domain. Never NC, ND, Sampling+ or custom licenses.
  • Music out: CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • No pitch-shifting. Time-stretch is fine, and so are other hacks: trim, fade, filter, reverb, delay.
  • Credit travels in the record.

The record

Each Performance lists every sound with its source, license, author and checksum, plus every setting and the seed. Anyone can check the credits or rebuild the Performance exactly. How a record is built.
