Live data: EarthScope SeedLink (rtserve.earthscope.org), Global Seismographic Network (IU), vertical broadband channel. The window of recent ground motion is detrended, mirrored so it loops without a click, scaled to a steady level and played as a band-limited wavetable. Offline, the page plays ten minutes recorded at Albuquerque (IU ANMO) on 27 Feb 2010.
